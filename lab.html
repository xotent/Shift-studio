IDENTITY STUDY

Signal Form

Signal Form explores how a simple set of graphic rules can create a recognizable, adaptable presence across changing formats. The direction prioritises clarity, space, and a confident visual pulse.

DIGITAL PRODUCT

Common Thread

Common Thread is a wayfinding exercise for digital spaces with several related paths. It tests a clearer hierarchy, useful signposting, and small moments of continuity between sections.

CAMPAIGN SYSTEM

Open Circuit

Open Circuit considers a campaign system that can be recognisable without becoming repetitive. The visual language uses open shapes, structured copy areas, and adaptable compositions.

EDITORIAL SPACE

Field Notes

Field Notes is an editorial system for collecting observations, thinking in public, and building a useful trail of ideas. It balances strong hierarchy with a more relaxed reading rhythm.

INTERACTION STUDY

Soft Focus

Soft Focus looks at interaction patterns that signal change without demanding constant attention. The study focuses on reduced visual noise, deliberate pacing, and clear user feedback.

WEB CONCEPT

Next Surface

Next Surface tests a modular web layout with a bold visual centre and practical content structure. The direction is designed to flex between concise updates and deeper stories.
