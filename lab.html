TYPE SYSTEM / CONCEPT

Variable Signals

Variable Signals asks how a flexible typographic voice can carry both information and feeling. The study tests expressive scale, tight spacing, and a modular set of graphic interruptions designed to make editorial moments feel alive while keeping everyday reading clear.

DIGITAL PRODUCT / IN PROGRESS

Small Wins

Small Wins is a product concept focused on gentle momentum. It explores an interface that gives people room to set small intentions, notice their progress, and return without punishment when life interrupts the plan.

INTERACTION STUDY / EXPLORATION

Soft Motion

Soft Motion explores interaction patterns that support navigation and feedback through restraint. The question is simple: how can motion help people understand a system without asking them to stop and watch it?

BRAND WORLD / CONCEPT

After Hours

After Hours is a brand-world study about giving digital identity a more physical pulse. It combines calm surfaces, deliberate contrast, and a visual language that can feel composed in daylight and more expressive after dark.

CAMPAIGN PROTOTYPE / IN PROGRESS

First Move

First Move tests campaign messaging and touchpoints around a familiar problem: people often know change would help, but not where to begin. The prototype explores how a clearer invitation can turn hesitation into a useful next step.

FUTURE WEB / EXPLORATION

Open Surface

Open Surface explores an adaptive approach to web composition. Instead of treating every screen as a fixed canvas, it investigates layouts that respond to the shape of content, the pace of reading, and the space a visitor has available.
